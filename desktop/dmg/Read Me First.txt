Kontext Studio - macOS
======================

1. Drag "Kontext Studio" into the Applications folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click Kontext Studio.

This beta is not yet notarized by Apple.

If macOS says:

  "Apple could not verify 'Kontext Studio' is free of malware that may harm
   your Mac or compromise your privacy."

then macOS blocked the first launch. Allow this copy once:

  a. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  b. Scroll to Security and click "Open Anyway" next to Kontext Studio.
  c. Confirm, then open Kontext Studio again within about an hour.

Or run this in Terminal:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Kontext Studio.app"

Only use these steps for the copy you downloaded from:

  https://github.com/kontext-tech/kontext-studio/releases

Your projects and settings live in ~/.kontext.
Logs live in ~/Library/Logs/Kontext Studio.
